Automation 2

Automatic billing over WhatsApp

Every morning, the system generates the month's invoices and automatically notifies whoever is close to or past the due date.

At most two messages per invoice

2 days before due

Reminder of the amount and due date

On the day

Invoice due date

1 day later, if unpaid

A single late reminder

After that the automation never repeats — so it doesn't bother the client.

What goes in the message

Everything the person needs to pay, in a single message

  • Person's name and reference month
  • Exact amount and due date
  • Pix key to pay directly — no link, no fee
  • Request for proof of payment, in the same conversation
Hi, Marcos! Your September membership is due on 09/30, for R$220.00.
Pix key: [parking lot's tax ID]
Once you pay, just send the proof of payment right here.

Payment confirmation is always done by a human

Customer pays and sends the proof of payment in the chat
→
The manager checks the proof or the bank statement
→
Marks the invoice as paid, with date and amount

There's no automatic payment gateway — the final word is always whoever checks it. You can even log it when someone pays two months at once.

One switch turns everything on or off

If you need to pause the messages — automatic or manual — just flip that switch. Nothing technical involved. Right now it's on.
